01 · About

About

I work on one problem: how artificial intelligence can give every student a personal tutor and every teacher superpowers.

Born and raised in Morocco and based in San Francisco.

As Founder & CEO of LiveTech.AI, I lead an AI-powered learning company building personalized learning paths, AI tools that save teachers hours every week, and real-time school analytics. Our platforms serve national ministries of education and school districts — and I advise ministries of education on AI and digital transformation.

Before LiveTech, I built some of the world's most-taken AI courses with DeepLearning.AI and Andrew Ng, including the Deep Learning and Natural Language Processing specializations on Coursera.
